TEAM SPARK

YOU HAVE THE DREAM, NOW FIND THE TEAM

Big ideas deserve great teammates. TeamSpark helps students discover teammates who share their interests, build teams for academic competitions, and connect safely with parent approval.

How TeamSpark Works

1. Explore

Tell TeamSpark what you enjoy and discover competitions that match your interests.

2. Find Teammates

Discover students with shared interests and complementary skills.

3. Connect Safely

Get parent approval and communicate using safe, predefined messages.
